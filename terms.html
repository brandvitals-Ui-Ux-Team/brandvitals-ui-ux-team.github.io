Terms of Service

Plain-language terms for our website, services and products. Where you have a signed contract with us, it comes first.

Last updated: October 2026

1. About these terms

These terms cover brandvitals.io and the services and products of Brand Vitals: the agency, Vitals Frames, Vitals Web, Vitals Stage, Vitals GO, Vitals Crew and Vitals Hire. Where you have a signed proposal or contract with us, that document comes first.

2. Agency and studio services

  • Scope, deliverables, timelines, revision rounds and fees are set in each proposal.
  • Work outside the agreed scope is quoted before it starts.
  • Final files and usage rights pass to you once the related invoice is paid, unless the proposal says otherwise.
  • Ad spend is paid to the ad platforms and is separate from our fees.

3. Vitals GO

  • The workspace owner is responsible for who has access to the workspace and for the content uploaded to it.
  • Your files and your clients' data stay yours. We use them only to run the service.
  • Review links are private. Share them only with the people who should approve the work.
  • Plans, limits and billing are shown when you subscribe. You can stop at the end of any billing period.

4. Vitals Crew

  • Brand Vitals acts as the intermediary between clients and vetted freelancers or teams, and may also propose its own in-house team, labelled as such.
  • Clients pay the agreed price plus a 20% service fee. Payment is held until the client approves the delivery.
  • If our quality review returns a delivery three times, or the client rejects two deliveries as off-brief, we switch to another member at our cost and the client's deadline does not move.
  • Rights in the final work pass to the client on payment. Clients and members agree not to move a job found on Vitals Crew off the platform.

5. Vitals Hire

  • Creating a profile and applying is free for candidates.
  • Every job must show a salary range. Jobs may not exclude people on grounds the law prohibits; roles reserved for Saudi nationals under Saudization rules must be marked as such.
  • Our screening is a professional opinion to save time. The hiring decision, the offer and the employment contract are between the company and the candidate.
  • Companies use candidate data only for the job the candidate applied to.

6. Payments and taxes

We invoice in Egyptian pounds, Saudi riyals or US dollars, as agreed. Value-added tax is added where the law requires it. Invoices are due on the dates set in the proposal.

7. Results

Figures and case results on this site describe specific past engagements. They are not a promise of the same result for another business.

8. Website content

Text, brand assets and design on this site belong to Brand Vitals and may not be reproduced without permission.

9. Liability

We do our work with care, but we are not liable for indirect losses or for the decisions of ad platforms, payment providers or other third parties. Our total liability on any engagement is limited to the fees paid for it, unless your contract says otherwise.

10. Governing law and changes

The governing law and place of disputes are set in your signed proposal or contract. We may update these terms; the date at the top shows the latest version.
