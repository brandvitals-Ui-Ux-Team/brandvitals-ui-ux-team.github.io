Contribution margin, not ROAS alone

ROAS counts revenue, but an online store lives on what is left after product, delivery, payment fees and returns. Two campaigns with the same ROAS can make very different profit if one sells low-margin items. E-commerce marketing at Brand Vitals sets the target cost per order from contribution margin, product by product, and reviews it whenever prices or costs move.

Cash on delivery and real sales

In Egypt many orders are paid in cash at the door, and some are never confirmed or are returned. Ad platforms count the order the moment it is placed. Brand Vitals tracks confirmed and delivered orders where possible and sends them back to the platforms, so campaigns learn from customers who actually paid.

Hero products and retention

Spreading ads across a whole catalogue usually spreads results thin. Focusing paid media on a few hero products, each with its own creative and landing page, gives the algorithm a clear signal, while a smaller catalogue campaign keeps the rest selling. Email, SMS and WhatsApp flows then turn first orders into repeat customers.

Key terms

Terms used on this page.

Contribution margin
Contribution margin is what is left from a sale after product, delivery, payment and return costs. Targets set on it stay honest when prices and costs move.
AOV (average order value)
AOV is revenue divided by the number of orders. Bundles, delivery thresholds and upsells raise it, which lets you afford a higher cost per order.
LTV (customer lifetime value)
LTV is the total margin a customer brings over all their orders. Brands with repeat buyers can pay more for the first order because the later ones carry the profit.
Cash on delivery (COD)
Cash on delivery means the customer pays the courier when the order arrives. It is common in Egypt, and it means a placed order is not yet revenue until it is delivered and paid.
Creative fatigue
Creative fatigue is when the same audience has seen an ad too often and results drop. A steady supply of new variations is the cure, not a bigger budget.
Retargeting
Retargeting shows ads to people who visited, watched or added to cart. Its ROAS often looks high because many of those people would have bought anyway.
