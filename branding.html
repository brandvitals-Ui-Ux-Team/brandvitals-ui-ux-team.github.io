Arabic and English, designed together

Many identities are designed in English and given an Arabic version at the end, which shows in uneven weights and awkward lockups. Brand Vitals designs the Arabic and English logo, type and layouts together, so the brand looks like itself on a Cairo billboard, a Riyadh storefront and an English pitch deck.

From identity to campaigns

An identity only pays back when it is used every week. Brand Vitals hands over guidelines and source files you own, then produces the launch campaign and ad templates with Vitals Frames, so the new brand reaches customers instead of staying in a PDF.

Pricing

How branding is priced.

Brand projects are priced per project by custom proposal, with the number of concepts and revision rounds written in. Every engagement is scoped in a written proposal with deliverables, timeline, revision rounds and a fixed fee. Media budgets are paid straight to the ad platforms, separate from our fees. Invoices are in Egyptian pounds, Saudi riyals or US dollars, and VAT is added where the law requires it. Anything outside the agreed scope is priced and approved before it starts.

Key terms

Terms used on this page.

Brand positioning
Positioning is the one idea a brand wants to own in the customer's mind, against named alternatives. Every message, visual and offer should be checked against it.
Brand guidelines
Brand guidelines set how the logo, colours, type, tone of voice and imagery are used, in Arabic and English, so every team and agency produces work that looks like one brand.
Creative testing
Creative testing runs several hooks, angles and formats against the same audience and budget rules, so the winner is chosen by results instead of taste.
UGC (user-generated content)
UGC-style ads are filmed the way customers and creators film themselves. They feel native to the feed and are cheap to produce in many variations for testing.
Creative fatigue
Creative fatigue is when the same audience has seen an ad too often and results drop. A steady supply of new variations is the cure, not a bigger budget.
