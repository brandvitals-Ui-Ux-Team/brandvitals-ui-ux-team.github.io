What we do

Organization, management and production, under one team.

01 · Organization

Event organization

The plan behind the event, built before anything is booked.

  • Planning and master timeline
  • Venue sourcing and contracts
  • Supplier and vendor management
  • Guest lists, invitations and registration
  • Budget control and reporting
02 · Management

Event management

The team that runs the day, minute by minute.

  • On-site operations team
  • Run of show and stage management
  • Guest flow, hospitality and VIPs
  • Supplier coordination on the day
  • Contingency and safety planning
03 · Production

Event production

Everything guests see and hear, built to spec.

  • Stage design and fabrication
  • Sound, lighting and LED screens
  • Exhibition booth design and build
  • Branding, signage and printing
  • Technical rehearsals
04 · Reporting

Coverage and reporting

Proof of what happened, and what it achieved.

  • Photo and video coverage
  • Live social updates
  • Attendance and feedback report
  • Post-event content package
