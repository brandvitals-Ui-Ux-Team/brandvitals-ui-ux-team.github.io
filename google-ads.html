Why Arabic search needs its own keyword list

People in Egypt and Saudi Arabia search the same product with different words, in Arabic script, in English and sometimes in Latin letters. A keyword list translated from English misses most of that demand and pays for irrelevant clicks. Google Ads at Brand Vitals starts with bilingual keyword research for each market, then groups searches by intent so every ad and landing page answers the question that was typed.

Performance Max, used carefully

Performance Max can find buyers across Google's inventory, but it also spends on searches for your own brand and on placements that rarely convert. Brand Vitals turns it on only when conversion data is clean, excludes brand terms where needed, and reads results next to search campaigns before shifting budget.

Pricing

How Google Ads is priced.

Google Ads management is scoped as part of a Performance Sprint or an always-on programme, priced by custom proposal. Every engagement is scoped in a written proposal with deliverables, timeline, revision rounds and a fixed fee. Media budgets are paid straight to the ad platforms, separate from our fees. Invoices are in Egyptian pounds, Saudi riyals or US dollars, and VAT is added where the law requires it. Anything outside the agreed scope is priced and approved before it starts.

Key terms

Terms used on this page.

Performance Max
Performance Max is a Google Ads campaign type that buys across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display and Maps from one budget. It needs clean conversion data and brand exclusions to stay efficient.
Negative keywords
Negative keywords stop search ads from showing for searches you do not want, such as jobs, free or a competitor's support line. Reviewing them weekly protects the budget.
CPA (cost per acquisition)
CPA is what you pay in ads for one result you care about: a purchase, a qualified lead or a booking. It is the number to set against margin or deal value.
Conversion rate
Conversion rate is the share of visitors or clicks that complete the action you want, such as a purchase or a form. Speed, trust and the offer move it more than design alone.
Offline conversions
Offline conversions send outcomes that happen outside the website, such as a closed deal in the CRM or a paid cash-on-delivery order, back to the ad platforms.
Landing page
A landing page is built for one campaign and one action. It repeats the ad's promise, answers the main objections and removes every link that does not lead to the action.
