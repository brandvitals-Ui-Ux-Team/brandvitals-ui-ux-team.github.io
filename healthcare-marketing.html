Bookings, not inquiries

A cheap form fill means little if the patient never books. Healthcare marketing at Brand Vitals connects every lead source, WhatsApp, calls and forms, to the CRM or booking system, then optimizes campaigns toward bookings and reports the inquiry-to-booking rate by channel.

Compliant creative for a sensitive category

Meta, Google, TikTok and Snap all restrict health advertising, and careless campaigns get rejected or limited. Brand Vitals plans targeting and messaging to stay inside platform and medical guidelines: clear, honest claims, no before-and-after promises, and landing pages that explain the service and the next step.

Budget that follows capacity

Each specialty and branch has its own demand, margin and free slots. Pushing ads to a fully booked clinic wastes money while another branch waits for patients. Campaigns at Brand Vitals are split by service line and branch, so budget moves toward the capacity and margin that need it.

Key terms

Terms used on this page.

CPA (cost per acquisition)
CPA is what you pay in ads for one result you care about: a purchase, a qualified lead or a booking. It is the number to set against margin or deal value.
Offline conversions
Offline conversions send outcomes that happen outside the website, such as a closed deal in the CRM or a paid cash-on-delivery order, back to the ad platforms.
Conversion rate
Conversion rate is the share of visitors or clicks that complete the action you want, such as a purchase or a form. Speed, trust and the offer move it more than design alone.
Consent mode
Consent mode adjusts Google tags to each visitor's cookie choice. Tracking respects the choice while modelled data fills some of the gap.
Click-to-WhatsApp ads
Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a chat with the business instead of a website. They suit markets where customers want to ask before buying, as long as replies are fast and tracked.
