Speed and trust before design

Most visitors from ads arrive on a phone, often on a mobile connection. A page that loads slowly or hides the price and the next step loses them before the design is even seen. Brand Vitals starts CRO with speed, clear offers and trust signals in both languages, then tests layout and copy once the basics hold.

Website design in Egypt, with Arabic first-class

Arabic pages are designed right-to-left from the start, with their own copy and their own URL, not mirrored from English at the end. That matters for customers, who notice awkward Arabic, and for search, which ranks each language separately.

Key terms

Terms used on this page.

CRO (conversion rate optimization)
CRO is the practice of finding where visitors drop off and testing changes that help more of them buy or enquire, so the same traffic brings more customers.
A/B test
An A/B test splits visitors between two versions of a page and compares results. It needs enough conversions per version before the difference can be trusted.
Landing page
A landing page is built for one campaign and one action. It repeats the ad's promise, answers the main objections and removes every link that does not lead to the action.
Core Web Vitals
Core Web Vitals are Google's measures of loading speed, responsiveness and layout stability on real devices. Slow pages waste ad spend before anyone reads the offer.
Conversion rate
Conversion rate is the share of visitors or clicks that complete the action you want, such as a purchase or a form. Speed, trust and the offer move it more than design alone.
hreflang
hreflang tags tell search engines which page is the Arabic version and which is the English one, so each searcher lands on the right language.
