Bilingual architecture comes first

Many bilingual sites in the region show Arabic and English on the same address, switched by a button. Search engines then see one page and rank it in one language. Brand Vitals gives each language its own URL, links them with hreflang, and writes Arabic pages natively instead of translating them, so each version can rank on its own.

Ready for AI search

Answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews quote pages that answer clearly, define their terms and state facts the same way everywhere. Brand Vitals writes answer-first summaries, self-contained FAQs and consistent company details, and keeps a public llms.txt so these systems understand the business.

Key terms

Terms used on this page.

Technical SEO
Technical SEO makes a site easy to crawl, index and understand: clean URLs, speed, structured data, sitemaps and no duplicate pages competing with each other.
hreflang
hreflang tags tell search engines which page is the Arabic version and which is the English one, so each searcher lands on the right language.
Local SEO
Local SEO helps a business appear in map and near-me searches through a verified Google Business Profile, consistent name, address and phone, and genuine reviews.
AI search optimization (GEO)
AI search optimization prepares content to be found, understood and quoted by answer engines such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews: clear answers, defined terms and consistent facts.
Core Web Vitals
Core Web Vitals are Google's measures of loading speed, responsiveness and layout stability on real devices. Slow pages waste ad spend before anyone reads the offer.
Landing page
A landing page is built for one campaign and one action. It repeats the ad's promise, answers the main objections and removes every link that does not lead to the action.
