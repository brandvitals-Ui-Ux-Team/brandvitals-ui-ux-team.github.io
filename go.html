01 · How it works

Send it. They say GO. You get paid.

That is the whole product. Everything else in Vitals GO exists to make one of these three steps faster.

  1. 1

    Send it

    Pick the client, drop the file. They get one link on WhatsApp, email or SMS. No account for them.

  2. 2

    They say GO

    One tap to approve, or a pin on the exact spot to change. Every answer is saved with their name and the time.

  3. 3

    You get paid

    “One small change” outside the agreement becomes a priced request. They approve it, it becomes an invoice.
