Qualified leads over cheap leads

A low cost per lead looks good in a report until the sales team cannot reach or close anyone. Real estate marketing at Brand Vitals asks the questions that matter in the form itself, such as budget, timeline and unit type, and sends sales outcomes from the CRM back to the ad platforms so they learn what a good lead looks like.

A project people remember

Renders and price lists rarely make a project memorable, because every competitor shows the same. Brand Vitals starts with positioning, a clear story for who the project is for and why it is worth the price, then produces the film and creative with Vitals Frames to sell the lifestyle, not only the unit.

Follow-up within the hour

Property leads go cold in hours when nobody replies. Campaigns are only as good as the follow-up behind them, so Brand Vitals agrees reply times with the sales team, routes WhatsApp and form leads to one owner, and reports follow-up speed next to cost per lead.

Key terms

Terms used on this page.

CPA (cost per acquisition)
CPA is what you pay in ads for one result you care about: a purchase, a qualified lead or a booking. It is the number to set against margin or deal value.
Offline conversions
Offline conversions send outcomes that happen outside the website, such as a closed deal in the CRM or a paid cash-on-delivery order, back to the ad platforms.
Brand positioning
Positioning is the one idea a brand wants to own in the customer's mind, against named alternatives. Every message, visual and offer should be checked against it.
Click-to-WhatsApp ads
Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a chat with the business instead of a website. They suit markets where customers want to ask before buying, as long as replies are fast and tracked.
Landing page
A landing page is built for one campaign and one action. It repeats the ad's promise, answers the main objections and removes every link that does not lead to the action.
