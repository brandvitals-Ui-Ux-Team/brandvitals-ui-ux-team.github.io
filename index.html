07 · Where we work

Two home markets. One team that knows both.

Egypt and Saudi Arabia buy differently, speak differently and pay differently. We plan for each one, not for “MENA” in general.

Egypt
Where people are
Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp, with Google for search-led categories
How they buy
They ask on WhatsApp before buying; cash on delivery, InstaPay, cards and installments
Language
Egyptian Arabic for most brands, English for premium and B2B
What we watch
Prices that move every few months, so targets are set on margin, not a fixed ROAS
Read: marketing in Egypt
Saudi Arabia
Where people are
Snapchat, TikTok, Instagram, X and YouTube, with Google for high-intent search
How they buy
Mada, Apple Pay and buy-now-pay-later; prices shown in riyals with 15% VAT included
Language
Saudi Arabic and local faces; content written for elsewhere is noticed fast
What we watch
Ramadan, Founding Day, National Day, White Friday and payday around the 27th
Read: Saudi Arabia's calendar

Same working days in both: Sunday to Thursday. Invoices in EGP, SAR or USD.
