Why paid social is measured on margin

A campaign can show a healthy ROAS in Ads Manager and still lose money. That happens when the ROAS target ignores product cost, delivery, returns and cash-on-delivery orders that are never paid. Brand Vitals sets the target from your contribution margin, so a good week in Ads Manager is also a good week in the bank account. In Egypt, where prices move every few months, the target is reviewed whenever prices or costs change.

Creative is the main lever

Targeting options on Meta, TikTok and Snapchat have narrowed, so the ad itself now decides who sees it and who buys. Paid social at Brand Vitals runs a weekly testing rhythm: new hooks, angles and formats briefed with Vitals Frames, judged on cost per result rather than likes. Winning ideas get variations before they fatigue; losing ones are switched off with a note on why.

Pricing

How performance marketing is priced.

Performance marketing is usually run as a Performance Sprint or an always-on programme, priced by custom proposal. Every engagement is scoped in a written proposal with deliverables, timeline, revision rounds and a fixed fee. Media budgets are paid straight to the ad platforms, separate from our fees. Key terms

Terms used on this page.

ROAS (return on ad spend)
ROAS is revenue from ads divided by what the ads cost. A ROAS of 3 means every 1 spent brought back 3 in sales. ROAS says nothing about profit on its own, so read it next to your margin.
Break-even ROAS
Break-even ROAS is one divided by your gross margin. With a 40% margin it is 2.5: below that, ads lose money on every sale. Vitals Forecast shows it first for this reason.
CPA (cost per acquisition)
CPA is what you pay in ads for one result you care about: a purchase, a qualified lead or a booking. It is the number to set against margin or deal value.
Conversions API
Conversions API sends conversion events from your server straight to the ad platform. It keeps working when browsers block the pixel, so the algorithm learns from real outcomes.
Creative testing
Creative testing runs several hooks, angles and formats against the same audience and budget rules, so the winner is chosen by results instead of taste.
Click-to-WhatsApp ads
Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a chat with the business instead of a website. They suit markets where customers want to ask before buying, as long as replies are fast and tracked.
