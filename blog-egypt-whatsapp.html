WhatsApp is your sales funnel in Egypt

Most Egyptian customers want to ask before they buy. How to turn WhatsApp from a messy inbox into a measured channel.

In Egypt many customers ask on WhatsApp before they buy, so WhatsApp works as part of the sales funnel. Treat it as a channel with targets: fast first replies, prepared answers with prices and delivery times, every conversation tracked to the ad that started it, and reporting on sales, not chats.

Last updated 16 October 2026

Click-to-WhatsApp ads often bring the cheapest conversations in Egypt. Without a process behind them, most of those conversations never become sales, and nobody can tell which ad worked.

Answer in minutes, not hours

Reply speed is the biggest driver of conversion on WhatsApp. Set working hours, an instant greeting with prices and the next step, and a target first reply under ten minutes.

Track every conversation

  • Use a WhatsApp Business API inbox so the whole team shares one number.
  • Tag each chat with the ad it came from and its outcome.
  • Send sales back to Meta as conversions so the algorithm finds more buyers.

Write replies that close

Prepare short answers to the ten questions you get most, with photos, prices, delivery times and a clear way to pay, including cash on delivery and InstaPay.

Set up WhatsApp Business properly

Use a WhatsApp Business account with the business name, hours, address and catalogue filled in. Set an instant greeting that confirms you received the message, gives prices or a link to them, and states the next step. Set an away message for outside working hours that says when someone will reply.

If more than one person answers, give each conversation one owner and a short label such as new, waiting for payment, delivered or lost. A shared phone with no owner is where most leads disappear.

Track every conversation back to the ad

Click-to-WhatsApp ads already know which ad opened the chat. Keep that link: use a different pre-filled first message or reference for each campaign, record the source when the conversation becomes an order, and send confirmed sales back to the ad platform as offline conversions. Then the algorithm learns from buyers, not from people who only said hello.

Website WhatsApp buttons should be tracked as events too, with the page they were clicked on, so you can see which pages start conversations.

Measure the funnel, not the inbox

  • Conversations started: how many chats each campaign opened.
  • First reply time: how long until a person answered, during working hours.
  • Conversation to order: the share of chats that became orders.
  • Delivered and paid: orders that arrived and were paid, especially with cash on delivery.

Replies that close

Prepare short answers to the questions you get most: price, sizes or options, delivery time and cost, how to pay, and how returns work. Add a photo or a short video for each main product. End every answer with one clear next step, such as confirming the address or choosing a payment method, including cash on delivery and InstaPay.

Who answers, and when

Most lost WhatsApp sales are lost in the first hour. Decide who covers each part of the day, including evenings when many people browse, and who takes over when the first person is busy. If the team is small, narrow the hours you promise and answer fast within them, rather than promising all day and answering late.

Following up without spamming

A conversation that stops is not always a lost sale. A short follow-up the next day, answering the question the customer was probably weighing, such as delivery time, size or payment, often brings it back. Keep it to one or two follow-ups, stop when the customer says no, and never add people to broadcast lists they did not ask for.

From conversation to delivered order

With cash on delivery, the sale is not finished when the customer says yes. Confirm the address and the order details in writing, send a short message when the order ships, and record whether it was delivered and paid. That last step is what turns WhatsApp reporting from a count of chats into a measure of revenue, and it is the number worth sending back to the ad platforms.

Connecting WhatsApp to your CRM

As volume grows, a phone is not enough. A WhatsApp Business API setup connected to a CRM or a shared inbox lets several people answer from one number, keeps the history of every customer, records the campaign that started each chat and lets you report on sales per source. It also makes it possible to send confirmed orders back to the ad platforms automatically.

Start simple: one number, clear labels for each stage, and a weekly look at conversations started, first reply time and orders. Add tools when the process is clear, not before, so the software follows how your team actually sells.

The takeaway

Treat WhatsApp as a channel with targets and reporting, not a phone in someone's pocket.

Key terms

Terms used in this article.

Click-to-WhatsApp ads
Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a chat with the business instead of a website. They suit markets where customers want to ask before buying, as long as replies are fast and tracked.
Offline conversions
Offline conversions send outcomes that happen outside the website, such as a closed deal in the CRM or a paid cash-on-delivery order, back to the ad platforms.
Cash on delivery (COD)
Cash on delivery means the customer pays the courier when the order arrives. It is common in Egypt, and it means a placed order is not yet revenue until it is delivered and paid.
Conversion rate
Conversion rate is the share of visitors or clicks that complete the action you want, such as a purchase or a form. Speed, trust and the offer move it more than design alone.
